# Supplier orders, reception and supplier pendings

Objective: stock enters only through supplier orders that wait in a queue until received and verified; damaged/expired and missing boxes become pendings the supplier settles by replacement or write-off; catalog holds the supplier cost (list − fixed discount − promotion) and loses the margin. Branch `feat/supplier-orders` (stacked on `feat/lot-pricing`).
Test runner: `bun test` in `abasto-web` against the isolated test database (`bun run db:up`).

## Specs

- S1: Remove margin — "quiero que quites margen en el catalogo". The product margin (UI, `product_margins`, the purchase price suggestion that used it) is removed. The standard sale price stays.
- S2: Supplier cost breakdown — "Ojo aqui no se tenia que manejar los precios de entrada, recuerda que eso es cuando se hace el pedido"; "El desglose"; "Si, se quedan en editar producto" (discounts). The list price per piece ("PRECIO X PZ") is entered per order line, prefilled from the last order line of that product. Fixed discount per piece and promotion per piece stay on the product ("Editar producto", section of the catalog form) because they are almost always fixed. Net cost per piece = list − discount − promotion (never negative); net cost per box = net per piece × pieces per box, rounded to cents (half up); line cost = net per box × boxes. Per-piece amounts keep 4 decimals. The order line stores the list price and the discount and promotion used, so later catalog changes do not alter past orders. The product form shows supplier code, fixed discount and promotion, and no list price.
- S3: Orders, not direct entries — "siempre se hacen por pedido y al llegar un pedido se hace la verificacion de que todo llego o si algo llego caducado ... casi es el mismo flujo solo queda en cola para verificar y confirmar la entrada del producto". Creating an order (supplier, warehouse, expected date, boxes per product of that supplier) moves no stock; it waits in a "Por recibir" queue with status Pedido; it can be cancelled before reception. The direct purchase entry is removed from the UI.
- S4: No sale price editing at entry — "tambien quitamos modificacion de precio cuando entra pedido de producto, solo se precarga y si el costo es superior aparece una advertensia para modificar el precio de salida"; user choice "A": the sale price is corrected in the catalog, not in the order. Sale price per box shows the standard price (read-only). When the net cost per box is above the standard price (or there is none), a warning tells the user to update the product's standard price in the catalog; it does not block.
- S5: Reception and verification — per line: good boxes, damaged/expired boxes, lot code and expiry; missing = ordered − good − damaged (never negative). Confirming creates one lot per line with good boxes (cost = cost per box × good boxes; sale price = the standard price at reception when it is ≥ the cost per box, else no own price so the lot follows the standard), records damaged and missing boxes as supplier pendings, and closes the order as Recibido (no further edits).
- S6: Supplier pendings — "si es asi se reporta como devolucion y un saldo que el proveedor nos debe"; settlement: "el saldo del proveedor se salda enviandonos el producto que no llego o en su defecto 'A lo chino' ya no se paga lo que nos quedaron a deber". Pendings are boxes per product valued at their cost, kept in an append-only ledger, shown per supplier. Settle by replacement (when receiving a later order of that supplier, replaced boxes enter as a lot at the original cost per box) or by write-off ("a lo chino": recorded as discounted from the payment, with its amount).
- S7: Out of scope — "no hay facturas no nos interesan"; "las tarimas son solo para el camion proveedor"; importing the supplier sheet ("implementando esto no habria caso de hacer hoja").
- S8: Existing data — purchases already recorded become Recibido with nothing lost; FIFO selling and per-lot price from `feat/lot-pricing` keep working.
- S9: Checks — rules covered by `bun test` through the public service interface; rejected input leaves stored data unchanged; `bun run web:lint`, `bun run web:check` (touched files), `bun run web:build` stay green.
